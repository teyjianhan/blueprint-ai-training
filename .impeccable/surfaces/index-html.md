# Surface: Landing page (index.html)

**Mode:** Persuade — the visitor decides and acts; design is the product.

**Scope:** The corporate site's home/landing page for Blueprint AI Training.

## Audience & job
HR / L&D decision-makers and business owners/directors at Malaysian companies, equal weight. Their job: confirm this HRDCorp provider is credible and practical, confirm claimability, then start a conversation. Employees are the learners, but this page sells to the decision-makers.

## Action
One conversion: tap through to **WhatsApp chat**. The WhatsApp number is pending from the founders — build the CTA with a placeholder link clearly marked for replacement. The utility band near the page's close is the dedicated conversion moment; the hero and nav each carry one red CTA pointing the same way.

## Proof & content
- HRDCorp registered / claimable status — lead proof; badge/statement placement early.
- Flagship: 2-day general AI workshop (aim: employees comfortable with AI; conducted with Claude — mention in description only, never in the course name).
- Coming soon: AI for Data Analysis, Advanced AI for Procurement, Advanced AI for Marketing — presented as upcoming, not bookable.
- Founder credentials (Tey Jian Han, Daniel Chua) and past clients/testimonials exist but content is pending — honest labelled placeholders, never fabricated specifics.
- No public pricing or dates; enquiry-based.

## Direction (pinned by founder)
BCG landing grammar executed at full fidelity, in the Bunga Raya palette (see DESIGN.md "The Bunga Raya Practice"): slim sticky nav → statement hero (full declarative headline + full-bleed imagery, one red CTA) → HRDCorp proof moment → editorial course card grid (eyebrow / title / description; flagship first, coming-soon cards visibly secondary) → practical-not-theory proof band → founders/credentials band → WhatsApp utility band → Midnight Navy footer.

**Memorable moment:** the first viewport — consulting-firm statement typography sharing the frame with bunga raya red; a Malaysian firm that looks like it belongs beside BCG.

## Constraints
- English only; copy must be easy for founders to hand-edit after launch.
- Craft bar: bcg.com's finish level. No AI clichés; corporate trust throughout.

## Unresolved
- WhatsApp number and contact details.
- Real founder bios, client names, testimonials, photography.
- Exact typefaces (DESIGN.md marks them provisional).
- Logo — none exists yet; wordmark treatment until one is made.
