Where this stands

The curriculum is largely designed. The problem is that it doesn't fit. Day 1 runs about 7h15 and Day 2 about 6h15, against roughly 12 teaching hours in a two-day container. Every round of editing has meant cutting good material to fit.

So the decision to take away is not what to teach. It's what container to teach it in. Three options are set out below, with a recommendation.

The unlock. The website is not a design exercise, it's a funnel. Problem pages and solution pages are conversion assets, and Meta ads on Day 2 route traffic into them. That single framing is what makes the two days one course rather than two topics sharing a room.

Settled

Locked

Decisions already taken. These don't need relitigating in the partner discussion.

Audience
All marketers, no narrowing. Performance, web and content people sit at cross-role tables, one business per table, so a designer and a performance marketer produce one campaign between them.
The spine
PASE — Practical, Action, Social, Emotional. Applied to page copy and CTAs on Day 1, to ad hooks and angles on Day 2. It appears on both days, which is what makes it read as the course's framework rather than a module.
The website
A funnel, built for conversion. Module renamed from "web design" to Build the funnel, so it's judged on whether it converts, not whether it's pretty.
Site structure
Fixed for everyone: home, problem pages, solution pages. Maximum six. Compressed version of the two-pillar architecture from the Chye proposal.
Page selection
The content schedule's top six Month 1 rows become the six pages built that afternoon. Prioritisation turns directly into what gets built.
SEO / AEO / GEO
One block, taught early, then written into a project instructions file that governs everything Claude Code generates. Pages come out compliant on the first pass instead of being fixed afterwards.
Keyword data
Ubersuggest excluded on cost. Google Keyword Planner is the free volume source — participants already create a Google Ads account for it. Free competitor signal comes from crawling rival sites for structure, titles, schema and content depth.
Meta
The agentic showpiece. Official Meta MCP builds the campaign, ad set, creative and ads. Everything paused by default, so no spend risk in the room.
Fallback account
Attendees without a Business Suite role use a Blueprint account. Recommendation on the table: create a separate empty training ad account with no payment method rather than exposing the live one.
Machines
Claude Code in the browser is the primary path, not the fallback — corporate laptops without admin rights will fail a local install, and every later module depends on setup succeeding.
GTM
Moved to Day 2, where the Meta Pixel and conversion tags give it a reason to exist. GA4 is hardcoded on Day 1.
Content course
Not removed — renamed. It's creative production, not content marketing. Gets 30 minutes as a taste on Day 2, which sells the deeper course.

Why Google before Meta. The manual, tedious platform goes while energy is high. Meta's agentic build then closes Day 2 the way the live URLs close Day 1. And it gives a clean narrative escalation: Google captures demand that already exists, but there's a hard ceiling on how much of it there is — Meta is how you create demand that doesn't.

Tool verification

Tested live

Checked against the real services rather than assumed. These results constrain what can actually be taught.

Meta official MCP

Viable

Meta-hosted remote server at https://mcp.facebook.com/ads. Claude and Claude Code are both supported clients. No Meta developer app required for the standard path — Facebook OAuth, minutes to connect. Registered here at user scope and confirmed connecting.

Per Meta's documentation it can pull reporting, create and edit campaigns, ad sets and ads, build creatives, upload image and video assets, manage custom audiences and catalogs, and run A/B tests. All ads are paused by default until manually set live, and account owners can block campaign creation or cap budgets from Business Suite → Settings → Integrations → Ads MCP server, enforced server-side.

Still untested: the write path on a live Malaysian account. Meta's docs carry a staged-rollout warning ("you may not have access to all tools yet"), so this must be proven before the curriculum depends on it.

Google Ads official MCP

Not workshop-viable

Google's server is a local Python process via pipx, not a hosted endpoint. Setup needs a Google Cloud project, the Google Ads API enabled, OAuth credentials or gcloud ADC, and a developer token with Explorer access — obtained through the API Center with unpredictable approval time.

It is also read-only. The entire toolset is search, get_resource_metadata and list_accessible_customers. No campaign creation. Google Ads via MCP is reporting only.

AdWhispr

Paid, and no Malaysia

Covers Meta, Google and TikTok with both research and execution, including launch_search_campaign and launch_pmax_campaign — genuine Google Ads write access, which the official Google server cannot do.

Two limits found by testing. Campaign execution is Pro-only; the free tier is research. And its keyword research, which pulls live Google Keyword Planner data and works well, does not support Malaysia. Supported geos are US, GB, CA, AU, DE, FR, ES, IT, NL, IN, BR, MX and JP. Verified in both directions with the same seed keywords.

Same cost objection as Ubersuggest, plus a geo gap on the half that would have mattered most here. Suitable as a "what a paid tool unlocks" demo, not as a required tool.

The decision to take away

Blocking

Roughly 13h30 of teaching against a 12-hour container. Pick the container and everything else follows.

Option A
2 days

Cut hard

Drop Google Ads entirely. Reduce the funnel build to customising a prepared template rather than building. One sale, cleanest brochure line.

Cost: the flagship deliverable weakens noticeably.

Option B
3 days

Premium intensive

Everything fits, one sale, one client relationship. Price it as an intensive rather than a padded two-day.

Cost: HR approves a three-day block far less readily than two days.

Option C · Recommended
2 + 2

Two courses

Split into AI for Web and Organic Growth and AI for Paid Marketing and Performance. Nothing gets cut. Four claim days instead of two, from the same client.

Cost: two sales instead of one.

The obvious worry is that a client buys A and never buys B, and that B depends on A for somewhere to send the ads. But that dependency is only about the workshop deliverable, not a real prerequisite — most companies already have a website. Course B opens with "bring your existing site and pixel access" and sells standalone. Both doors stay open.

Still open

Needs an answer

Google Ads — in or out?

No agentic path exists, so it's manual UI work, which is an energy drop after the Meta showpiece and breaks the "AI does the work" narrative. But search is the highest-intent traffic there is, and for the clinic-type businesses Blueprint serves, "chiropractor near me" is where the money is. Omitting it leaves a visible hole on the brochure.

Recommendation: include it, 45–60 minutes, reframed. Not a platform module — the earn versus buy decision. Claude splits yesterday's keyword list into what you earn and what you buy, writes the RSA assets from the buy list using PASE, and they build one paused Search campaign pointing at a Day 1 solution page. PMax gets ten minutes as a concept, not a build.

Own business or shared case study?

Own business is far better for the "applied to your own workflow" differentiator, but timing becomes unpredictable and some attendees arrive with no content, no logo and no clear idea. Cross-role tables push toward one business per table as the middle path.

Meta ad account for attendees without a role

Agreed that Blueprint's account is the fallback. Open: whether to create a separate empty training ad account with no payment method, rather than giving every attendee agent access to the live account with its real billing and campaign data.

Machines and pre-work

Own laptops with admin rights, or company-locked? The browser-first path covers most of this, but the pre-workshop setup session — the thing that reclaims 45 minutes on Day 1 — needs scheduling and a support channel.

Where do the scheduled tasks actually run?

A scheduled Claude task needs somewhere to execute after attendees leave the room. Claude Code's own scheduling, a cloud agent, or something on their machine that stops when the laptop closes? This changes what can honestly be promised.

To resume: load this file in a new session and say which container was chosen — A, B or C — and whether Google Ads is in. That's enough to draft the full outline against the right shape. The two reference artifacts sit in Downloads: chye-clinic-seo-proposal (1).html is the competitor dashboard target, Persistence-Chiropractic-Monthly-Content-Schedule.html is the content schedule target.

Also worth knowing: the Meta MCP write path is confirmed connecting but its campaign-creation path on a Malaysian account has not been proven yet. Test that before the curriculum commits to it as the Day 2 showpiece.
