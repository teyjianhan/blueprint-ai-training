Claude Mastery for Business Productivity and Automation

Two days that take a team from cautious to fluent. Day one is about getting exactly what you need out of a conversation. Day two is about never starting one from scratch again.

2 full days 8:30am to 5:30pm Theory + practical Laptops required 11 modules No coding

The course a company runs first

This is the programme that makes every later one land. It is built for whole companies and mixed departments starting from zero: the account manager, the HR executive, the finance and operations teams, the people whose week is emails, documents, spreadsheets and decisions.

Participants work with Claude, one of the world's leading AI assistants, from the first hour. Everyone brings a laptop and their own backlog, so every exercise below runs on your company's real work rather than our sample data.

Learning outcomes

What every participant can do by 5pm on day two

  • Brief AI properlyWrite a brief that gets a usable answer the first time, on the tasks their own role actually involves.
  • Analyse business dataTake a spreadsheet through to a written review with charts and recommendations they would put in front of a manager.
  • Think decisions throughUse structured frameworks with AI as a debate partner rather than an answer machine, and argue back when it is wrong.
  • Build working toolsProduce dashboards, calculators and small applications without writing a line of code.
  • Set up a system that persistsLoad their department's context once, teach AI their recurring procedures, and stop re-explaining themselves.
  • Handle data safelyKnow what should never go into a chat, how to check output, and where the limits sit.
The frame

Six jobs, taught in this order

Everything across the two days maps to one of six things AI does for a business. Participants place their own tasks against these on the first morning, and the last session walks back through all six.

01

Think

Pressure-test a decision before you commit to it.

02

Write

Proposals, reports, board papers and difficult emails.

03

Read

Long documents summarised, insights pulled out, meetings prepared for.

04

Analyse

Revenue, costs and patterns, read out of your own numbers.

05

Build

Dashboards, calculators and internal tools, with no code.

06

Research

Competitors, markets and customers, gathered at speed.

The two days

What actually happens in the room

Eleven modules across two days, each one taught and then practised before the room moves on. The timings are the designed shape of the programme rather than a fixed script: emphasis, examples and the departmental exercises are scoped with you before every run.

Day one

Master the conversation

"I can get exactly what I need out of AI, every time, in a single conversation."

Modules 1 to 5. By the end of the day every participant can open a blank chat, write a proper brief and finish a real deliverable in one sitting. No advanced features yet, just the conversation itself.

They also feel the friction of re-explaining their job from scratch every time. That is deliberate, and it is what day two fixes.

Day two

Master the system

"I never start from scratch again, and neither does my team."

Modules 6 to 11. Day one ended in a specific frustration: every new chat starts at zero. The morning names that problem and solves it, with persistent workspaces and reusable playbooks.

The afternoon widens what the room thinks is possible. By late afternoon they have built three working applications, which is not what anyone expected on Monday.

What we need from you

Every participant needs a laptop with an active Claude Pro subscription, logged in and tested before they arrive. Module 1 starts hands-on, so an account being set up in the room costs that person the first session. We work the setup through with your IT team ahead of the dates.

On the day: wifi that will hold the whole room, a projector, and some wall space.

More importantly, a few real documents and a sample of real performance data from each department. The exercises above only work because the data in them is yours. We send a short questionnaire once dates are set, and the sessions are scoped from the answers.
