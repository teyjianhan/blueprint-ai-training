Curriculum

What the two days cover

This is the working outline. We adjust the emphasis to your company before every run.

Day 1

Comfort and foundations

  1. What AI is (and what it isn't)

    A plain-language grounding. What today's AI does well, where it fails, and why nobody in the room is being replaced by it.

  2. First conversations

    Everyone gets hands on with Claude. Asking, refining, pushing back, treating AI like a capable junior instead of a search box.

  3. Delegating properly

    The skill that separates casual users from fluent ones: giving AI the context, examples and standards it needs to do real work.

  4. Your own backlog

    Participants pick a task from their actual week (a report, a difficult email, a summary) and finish it with AI before the day ends.

Day 2

Applied, by department

  1. Documents and writing

    Reports, proposals, minutes and translations, drafted, sharpened and checked against your company's tone.

  2. Analysis and decisions

    Reading long documents fast, comparing options, and preparing for meetings with a brief that took minutes instead of hours.

  3. Department deep-dives

    Breakout exercises tailored to the roles in the room (HR, finance, operations, sales) using scenarios from that function.

  4. Guardrails and habits

    Company data safety, checking AI output, and each participant leaving with a personal AI workflow for their role.

Group size, venue and scheduling are arranged per company. Message us and we'll work it out around your operations.

After the workshop

What your team takes away

  • Comfort replaces cautionPeople open an AI assistant the way they open email, without making it a big deal.
  • First drafts stop taking hoursReports, emails and summaries start from a competent draft instead of a blank page.
  • Every role has its use caseEach participant leaves with a workflow built for their own job, tested on their own tasks.
  • Judgement stays humanYour team knows what to check, what not to paste in, and where AI's limits are.
