Course overview

Two days that take a team from cautious to fluent

This is the programme a company runs first. It is built for whole companies and mixed departments starting from zero: the account manager, the HR executive, the finance and operations teams, the people whose week is emails, documents, spreadsheets and decisions. No coding, and no previous AI experience is assumed. Participants work with Claude, one of the world's leading AI assistants, from the first hour, on their own laptops and on a task from their own workload. Where the company approves it, the exercises run on its own material; where it does not, we supply realistic mock data and nothing sensitive leaves the room.

Day one is about getting exactly what you need out of a conversation: briefing AI properly, using it responsibly, teaching it your procedures as reusable Skills, and taking a spreadsheet through to a recommendation. Day two is about never starting from scratch again: a persistent Project for your department, whole tasks delegated to Claude Cowork, automations that run on a schedule, and working applications built without code. Every module is taught, then practised, before the room moves on, and participants leave with finished deliverables rather than a folder of notes.

Learning outcomes

Six jobs. What every participant can do by 5:30pm on day two

Read

A long report, contract or policy in; the parts that matter, the risks buried in it and the questions worth asking next, out.

Write

Brief AI properly and get a proposal, report or difficult email back at a standard they would put in front of a manager.

Analyse

Take a spreadsheet through to a written review with charts and recommendations, and know which numbers the argument rests on.

Automate

Load their department's context once, teach AI their procedures as Skills, delegate whole tasks to Cowork, and put the recurring ones on a schedule.

Think

Pressure-test a real decision from six perspectives, and argue back when the answer is wrong.

Build

Produce dashboards, calculators and small working applications without writing a line of code.
