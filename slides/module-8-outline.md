## Slide 2 — Section: Project vs. Cowork · Theory

Cowork: the shared team surface that sits alongside a personal Project, and which work belongs in which.

A Project (module 7) is deliberate and personal — one person's department context, loaded and configured by them. Cowork is the shared version: a space multiple people work in together, in the same session, rather than each running their own private conversation and comparing notes after.

(This is the third "which feature do I use" question of the day, after Project vs. single chat and Project vs. Memory in module 7 — worth the same comparison-slide treatment as those two.)
