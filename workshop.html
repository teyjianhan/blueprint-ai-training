Workshops

Training shaped to how your team actually learns

Some teams need to be convinced before they'll try. Some need to be shown. Some just need time to practise. We run all three, and the right one depends on where your people are today.

How we deliver

Three formats, one aim

Everything we run is built to end the same way: people reaching for AI on their own, without being told to. What changes is how much of the time is talking and how much is doing.

Briefing

Fully theory

  1. What it is

    A talk-led session with no laptops. We explain what AI is doing to work in Malaysia, what it means for your industry, and what a realistic plan looks like.

  2. Who it suits

    Leadership teams deciding whether to invest, or a whole company that needs the same starting picture before anyone is trained.

  3. Shape

    Short. Works with a large room. Often the step that gets budget approved for the hands-on training that follows.

Workshop

Theory + practical

  1. What it is

    The default. Each concept is explained, then practised immediately on the participant's own work, so nothing stays abstract.

  2. Who it suits

    Teams starting from zero. Most companies begin here, because most people have never been shown how to work with AI properly.

  3. Shape

    One to two days, laptops required, capped so every person gets attention. Everyone leaves having finished something real.

Lab

Fully practical

  1. What it is

    Almost no lecture. Your team brings live work and we sit with them while they do it, fixing what goes wrong as it goes wrong.

  2. Who it suits

    Teams already past the basics whose habits haven't stuck yet, or a department with one specific process they want rebuilt around AI.

  3. Shape

    Half-day sessions, often repeated. The format that turns a good training day into a change you can still see months later.

These stack. A briefing gets leadership aligned, a workshop trains the team, and labs keep it alive afterwards. Each one also stands on its own if that's all you need.

What we cover

The ground every format covers

Working with AI assistants Documents & writing Analysis & decisions Research & summarising Data safety & guardrails Checking AI's work

The emphasis is set per company. A finance-heavy team and a sales-heavy team leave with different sessions under the same headings.

Run on your work, not our examples

Participants bring their own reports, emails and spreadsheets. The exercises are your company's real tasks, which is why people keep using what they learned. They didn't practise on our sample data, they practised on Monday's actual job.

It's built for people who don't call themselves technical: the account manager, the HR executive, the finance team. No coding, anywhere. We're not a translated franchise or a licensed curriculum from somewhere else, and we don't hand out vendor certificates. What you get is a team that works differently.
