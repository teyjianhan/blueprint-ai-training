# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Two primary audiences, equal weight:

- **HR / L&D decision-makers** at Malaysian companies evaluating claimable training providers for their employees.
- **Business owners / directors** (often SMEs) deciding whether to invest in making their whole team AI-ready.

Their job on this site: confirm the provider is credible and practical, confirm HRDCorp claimability, and start a conversation. End learners are the employees who attend the workshops, but the website sells to the decision-makers.

## Product Purpose

Blueprint AI Training is a HRDCorp Training Provider founded by Tey Jian Han and Daniel Chua (both directors) to upskill Malaysians to be AI-ready. The website is a corporate site whose success is measured by qualified enquiries: convinced visitors tap through to **WhatsApp chat** (the single conversion action).

## Positioning

- **Practical, not just theory.** Courses are designed so employees leave able to actually use AI in their work; the founders aim to bring real value to organizations from each course.
- **HRDCorp registered training provider** — training is claimable under the HRD levy, a material factor in the Malaysian purchase decision.
- Core offer: the **2 Day AI-Ready Workshop** (public name since 2026-07-23; previously presented as "General AI Workshop"), whose main aim is making employees comfortable with AI. It is conducted with Claude — Claude may be mentioned in course descriptions, but course names and lead positioning stay on practical AI readiness, not a specific tool.
- **Never call it a "flagship"** (founder ruling, 2026-07-25): there is no flagship course. Describe the route instead: businesses most commonly send their team to the 2 day AI-Ready Workshop first, then go into specific department units. On the landing page, department destinations are shown as department names only, never as course names.
- **Differentiators (founder-stated, 2026-07-23):**
  1. The founders are AI engineers who came out of industry leading companies (companies not yet named publicly; keep general until they confirm).
  2. Unlike many training providers, they have actually implemented AI in corporate settings; keep this comparative claim general, never naming competitors.
  3. Sessions are designed so every participant brings home AI applied to their own workflow and use case.
  4. The curriculum suits both IT and non-IT backgrounds; no coding skills are required.

## Operating Context

- Malaysian corporate training market; HRD Corp levy claims are part of how buyers evaluate and justify training spend.
- Enquiry-based sales: **no public pricing or run dates.** Pricing and scheduling are handled per company after contact.
- Conversion path is WhatsApp-first, the common B2B contact channel in Malaysia.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Current offer: the 2-day AI-Ready Workshop (conducted with Claude).
- Coming soon: deeper specialist courses — e.g. AI for Data Analysis, Advanced AI for Procurement, Advanced AI for Marketing, with more planned. Present these as upcoming, not bookable.
- Language: **English only.**
- The founders will edit course details and copy manually after launch — keep content easy to locate and change in the codebase.
- **Undecided / pending from founders:** WhatsApp number and contact details; whether pricing/dates ever become public (site launches enquiry-based).

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **Blueprint AI Training.**
- No existing logo or typefaces. The founder pinned the visual direction, revised 2026-07-23 (v2, supersedes the bunga raya palette): **blue accents, not red**; layout and styling follow **today's bcg.com** as shown in the founder's screenshots (inset rounded panels, pill buttons, serif display, no full-width color bands); **Malaysian colors appear only inside gradient elements**, never as literal national imagery (no hibiscus, no flags). The team photo `assets/img/cover_group.png` is served as a full-frame webp at full resolution and must never be cropped (founder ruling 2026-08-14); the frame carries the founder's own arc art, and it anchors large to the band's bottom edge. This is the sanctioned exception to the no-full-width-bands rule; the inset grammar resumes below the hero. **Superseded in part (founder ruling 2026-08-16): the indigo header now runs on every page, not only home.** The header was always allowed to be full width, so the inset grammar is unaffected; what changed is that other pages no longer carry a white header, so the nav reads the same everywhere and home is distinguished by the hero band beneath it rather than by the bar itself.

- Course names must not include "Claude"; descriptions may mention it.

## Product Principles

1. **Practicality is the product.** Every claim ties to what employees can do after training — no AI hype.
2. **Claimability and credibility carry the sale.** Lead with HRDCorp status and real, verifiable proof.
3. **Lower the barrier to contact.** WhatsApp-first conversion with minimal friction everywhere.
4. **Speak to the buyer, empower the learner.** Decision-maker language, employee-outcome substance.
5. **Founder-editable content.** Details will change often; copy and course data must be simple to update by hand.
