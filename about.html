About us

We help Malaysian companies get comfortable with AI

Blueprint AI Training was started by two directors who kept seeing the same thing: companies buying AI tools that their people never really used.

Why we started this

The idea is simple to say and surprisingly hard to find: AI training that people still use after the course ends. Plenty of courses leave a team feeling inspired for a week. We'd rather they leave with habits, built on their own work, that survive contact with a normal Monday.

As an HRD Corp registered training provider, we build every course to be practical rather than theoretical. The gap in most Malaysian companies isn't knowledge about AI. It's comfort with it, and comfort is what the flagship two day workshop is designed to build.

The name is a small promise. A blueprint exists to be built from, and so do our courses.
