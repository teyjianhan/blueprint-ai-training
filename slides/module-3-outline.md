## Slide 6 — Section: AI hallucinations · Theory

Not in the source course outline — added as the direct justification for slide 7's checklist.

What a hallucination is: the model producing fluent, confident, wrong information with no built-in flag that it's uncertain. Plain-language cause for a business audience — it's predicting plausible next text, not looking anything up — without turning this into a technical detour.

Why it's here, not earlier: this is the risk the Responsible AI checklist's "verification of outputs" item exists to catch. Teaching the failure mode immediately before the fix, same shape as slide 2's Kahoot into slide 3's debrief.
