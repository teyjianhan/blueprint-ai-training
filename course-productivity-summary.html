Who it is for

The course a company runs first

Built for whole companies and mixed departments starting from zero: the account manager, the HR executive, the finance and operations teams, the people whose week is emails, documents, spreadsheets and decisions. Participants work with Claude from the first hour, and bring their own backlog, so every exercise runs on your company's own documents and numbers.

Learning outcomes

Brief AI properly

Write a brief that gets a usable answer the first time, on the tasks their role involves.

Analyse business data

Take a spreadsheet through to a written review with charts and recommendations they would put in front of a manager.

Think decisions through

Use structured frameworks to pressure-test a decision with AI, and argue back when it is wrong.

Day one

Master the conversation

Modules 1–5

"I can get exactly what I need out of AI, every time, in a single conversation."

By 5:30pm every participant can open a blank chat, write a proper brief and finish a deliverable in one sitting. They also feel the friction of re-explaining their job from scratch every time, which is deliberate, and is what day two fixes.

01

Introduction to AI and Claude for business strategy

70 min

Where generative AI helps a business, and what leaders need to understand about models, tokens and context to match capability to task. Introduces the Blueprint 6 framework that the whole two days map to.

02

Effective AI communication and strategic briefing

110 min

The five ingredients of a brief that works first time: Role, Context, Task, Format, Constraint. Practised across three business scenarios, then used to produce a data-informed recommendation from your own performance metrics.

03

AI governance, data security and responsible use

40 min

How to tell a legitimate AI tool from a data-harvesting one, and a classification framework for deciding what company information is safe to share. Closes on the Responsible AI checklist: verification, bias awareness and human oversight.

04

AI-powered business data analysis

65 min

The Analysis Ladder (Prepare, Describe, Diagnose, Prescribe), taken from raw spreadsheet through to strategic recommendation. Participants analyse a full performance dataset and produce an executive summary with recommendations.

05

Applied AI: individual business problem solving

150 min

An open session where everyone takes a task from their current workload through to a finished deliverable, then presents it to the room. Ends with an overnight assignment to audit their department for automation opportunities.

How the days are paced

Registration and networking from 8:30am, morning and afternoon tea breaks, and a full hour for lunch at noon on both days. Every module is followed by hands-on work on your own material, so the room is never listening for long.

Day two

Master the system

Modules 6–11

"I never start from scratch again, and neither does my team."

Welcome back and overnight debrief

45 min

Each HOD shares what their audit found: what can be automated, and what still needs a person to decide. The room then prioritises the opportunities worth doing first.

06

Strategic AI workspaces and persistent context

75 min

Why starting every chat from zero costs so much, and how a persistent workspace fixes it. Each participant builds a departmental workspace loaded with the department's KPIs, SOPs and brand guidelines.

07

Building reusable AI decision playbooks

75 min

Moving from ad-hoc outputs to standardised procedures using Skills. Each participant builds and validates two reusable playbooks for their workspace.

08

Interactive dashboard and report building

40 min

Building interactive dashboards from business data through conversation alone, from basic visualisations up to monitoring tools with filters, alerts and KPI scorecards.

09

AI-assisted strategic decision frameworks

35 min

Claude put to work as a debate partner, through Six Thinking Hats, on a decision each participant is facing now. Every claim has to cite a number.

10

No-code application building

90 min

Three working builds in ninety minutes through the Ideate, Iterate, Output framework: a task board, a document personalisation tool, and a weekly planner.

11

Capstone project and strategic deployment planning

80 min

Each participant presents their strongest deliverable, at a standard they could put in front of management, then leaves with a 30-day deployment plan and the Team Cascade Kit for training their team.
