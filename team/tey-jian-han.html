Jian Han is a co-founder and director of Blueprint AI Training. There is no bench of associate trainers behind him. If your company books a session, he is one of the two people who will be standing in the room.

Before Blueprint

He is an AI engineer by trade, and he did that work inside industry leading technology companies before he started doing it for Malaysian employers.

That is a different starting point from most training providers. He has had to make AI work against real company data, real deadlines, and real colleagues who never asked for any of it. A good deal of what he teaches comes out of that experience, including the unglamorous parts about what these tools are still bad at.

What he teaches

He runs the 2 day AI-Ready Workshop, the course most companies start with. It is conducted with Claude, and the aim is comfort rather than coverage. By the end of the two days, people should be reaching for AI on their own, without being told to.

The sessions are built so that every participant leaves with AI applied to their own work, not to a case study about a company they have never heard of. Finance people work on finance documents, HR people on HR letters. The material is written for IT and non-IT backgrounds alike, and no coding is required at any point.

What he writes about

His articles keep returning to one argument. AI readiness is a leadership decision rather than a software purchase, and the HRD levy your company has already paid is the budget for it. If you want a sense of how he thinks before you book anything, that is the place to start.
